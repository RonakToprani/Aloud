A new address, a much bigger shelf, and more coming every week.

Aloud has a new home

Hello, and thank you. You are one of the first people to read with Aloud, which I am genuinely happy about. Here is everything that has landed since you signed up.

A proper address

Aloud now lives at aloudreader.org. The old address still works, so nothing you saved has moved. If you keep Aloud on your home screen, it is worth adding it again from the new one.

Nearly 3,000 books, free

Open Browse classics and there is a whole library waiting, sorted by genre. They come from Project Gutenberg, the volunteer effort that has been digitising books out of copyright since 1971. Every one of them is read aloud exactly like a book you add yourself.

Signing in by email got easier

The email now brings a six-digit code as well as a link, so you can sign in on whichever device you are actually holding.

Silent reading is next

A silent reading mode is the next thing I am building, and I plan to keep shipping something most weeks for a while yet.

If there is something you wish Aloud did, hit reply and tell me. This early on, one message from a real reader changes more than anything else I could guess at.

Happy listening,
Ronak
