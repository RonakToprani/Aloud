Aloud has a new home

Hello, and thank you. You are one of the first people to read with
Aloud, which I am genuinely happy about. Here is everything that has
landed since you signed up.

A PROPER ADDRESS
Aloud now lives at https://www.aloudreader.org. The old address still works, so nothing you
saved has moved. If you keep Aloud on your home screen, it is worth
adding it again from the new one.

NEARLY 3,000 BOOKS, FREE
Open Browse classics (https://www.aloudreader.org/browse) and there is a whole library
waiting, sorted by genre. They come from Project Gutenberg, the
volunteer effort that has been digitising books out of copyright since
1971. Every one of them is read aloud exactly like a book you add
yourself.

SIGNING IN BY EMAIL GOT EASIER
The email now brings a six-digit code as well as a link, so you can
sign in on whichever device you are actually holding.

SILENT READING IS NEXT
A silent reading mode is the next thing I am building, and I plan to
keep shipping something most weeks for a while yet.

If there is something you wish Aloud did, hit reply and tell me. This
early on, one message from a real reader changes more than anything
else I could guess at.

Happy listening,
Ronak
